# Day Length

The **Day Length** mod by Yigit Guven lets you customize the length of Minecraft's day-night cycle to suit your playstyle. Whether you want longer days for building or extended nights for survival challenges, this mod puts full control in your hands. It's perfect for modpacks, servers, and casual play where time management is key.

---

## Installation

1. **Download the Mod**: Download the Day Length mod from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/yigit-guven/Day-Length/releases) or other official platforms.
2. **Install Minecraft**: Ensure you have the correct mod loader installed for your Minecraft version.
3. **Add Mod to the Mods Folder**: Place the Day Length mod into your Minecraft `mods` folder.
4. **Launch Minecraft**: Open the game using the mod loader profile and verify that Day Length is active in the Mods list.
